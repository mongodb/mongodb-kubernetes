* **Unified member-cluster RBAC**: Removed the `--member-cluster` flag from `kubectl mongodb multicluster generate-member-resources`. The command now renders member-cluster resources under fixed names — `mck-member-sa`, `mck-member-token`, `mck-member-role-base`, `mck-member-role-multicluster`, `mck-member-pvc-resize`, `mck-member-cluster-telemetry` (and their bindings), and the `mck-member-appdb`, `mck-member-database-pods`, and `mck-member-ops-manager` workload ServiceAccounts with the `mck-member-appdb` Role and RoleBinding — instead of per-cluster `mck-member-<cluster>-*` names, so a single render can be applied unchanged to every member cluster. Multi-cluster workload Pods on member clusters now run under the unified ServiceAccounts. When the member ServiceAccount is bound in more than one namespace (multiple `--workload-namespaces`, or a workload namespace different from `--member-cluster-namespace`), the three member roles `mck-member-role-base`, `mck-member-role-multicluster`, and `mck-member-pvc-resize` render as one namespaced Role (plus RoleBinding) per namespace rather than a ClusterRole with per-namespace RoleBindings; only `--operator-cluster-scoped` renders ClusterRoles, and `--operator-telemetry=false` is unchanged, rendering zero cluster-scoped resources. When upgrading, re-apply the output of `generate-member-resources` to each member cluster and delete the orphaned per-cluster `mck-member-<cluster>-*` resources. The `--member-cluster` flag of `kubectl mongodb multicluster generate-member-registration` is unchanged: the credential Secret and `MemberCluster` CR it emits are applied to the operator's cluster and remain named per member cluster.
